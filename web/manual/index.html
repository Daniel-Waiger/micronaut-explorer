Micronaut Planner

User Manual

This manual explains every screen in Micronaut Planner — a static, zero-install microscopy experiment planner that runs entirely in your browser — from opening the app for the first time to exporting a bench card for the microscope. No prior experience with the app is assumed: every term is defined the first time it matters, and every rule comes with a plain-language reason for why it works that way. Read the chapters in order if you're brand new, or jump straight to what you need with the search box or the quick-task list below.
